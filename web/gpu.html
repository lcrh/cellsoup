What this estimate means

Every two simulated seconds, the GPU records a coarse map of cell density, links, motion, recent activity, energy and reserves. Eleven bounded predictors compete to describe a 32-sample window. The smallest combined model and prediction code wins; the displayed estimate is that model's size in bits. Prediction error is checked on separate, unseen frames.

This is a limited observer, not a universal complexity score or a measure of intelligence. Simple repetition and noise should need small models. Population changes and physics can also create learnable patterns. The shuffled comparison preserves each channel's frequencies while removing spatial and temporal order.           Energy, storage & death
          

            

              Energy is short-lived and local. Actions and upkeep spend
              it, and it also decays. At zero the cell dies, even if it has
              stored reserves.
            

            

              photosynthesize r0 converts available sunlight into
              energy. It must be executed; idle cells absorb nothing. Each cell
              has a light-limited harvest budget per tick.
            

            

              Sunlight and activity generate heat. Crowded cells cool more
              slowly; linked cells share heat. Overheating drains usable energy.
              Use the Temperature view to see warm and overheated cells.
            

            

              store r0 amount creates stable reserves. Linked cells
              share storage. mobilize r0 amount turns reserves back
              into usable energy. These conversions are explicit actions.
            

            

              attack target amount costs energy and damages the
              target's energy. It transfers nothing. A corpse contains body
              material plus the victim's stored reserves;
              eat r0 eats a random corpse within reach and returns
              the energy gained. Gold rings mark successful feeding.
            

            

              sense r0 energy and
              sense r1 storage read your own pools.
              gradient r0 r1 senses sunlight.
              storage_gradient r0 r1 kind points toward reserves: 0
              living, 1 corpses, −1 both.
              peek r0 target alive distinguishes status.
            

          

        

        

          Assembly reference
          

            

              Eight registers, relative sensing and motion, at most 64
              instructions per genome. Target handles can be reused after a cell
              or corpse disappears. Division copies code exactly. Archive
              resampling is the mutation source.
