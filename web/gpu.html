What this estimate means

Every two simulated seconds, the GPU records a coarse map of cell density, links, motion, recent activity, energy and reserves. Eleven bounded predictors compete to describe a 32-sample window. The smallest combined model and prediction code wins; the displayed estimate is that model's size in bits. Prediction error is checked on separate, unseen frames.

This is a limited observer, not a universal complexity score or a measure of intelligence. Simple repetition and noise should need small models. Population changes and physics can also create learnable patterns. The shuffled comparison preserves each channel's frequencies while removing spatial and temporal order. Compare scores with the same sampling and model settings.

          Energy, storage & death
          

            

              Energy is short-lived and local. Actions and upkeep spend
              it, and it also decays. At zero the cell dies, even if it has
              stored reserves. A cell also dies when it reaches the configured
              lifespan, leaving a normal edible corpse. A lifespan of 0 disables
              death from old age.
            

            

              (photosynthesize) converts available sunlight into
              energy. It must be executed; idle cells absorb nothing. Each cell
              has a light-limited harvest budget per tick.
            

            

              Sunlight and activity generate heat. Crowded cells cool more
              slowly; linked cells share heat. Overheating drains usable energy.
              Use the Temperature view to see warm and overheated cells.
            

            

              (store amount) creates stable reserves. Linked cells
              share storage. (mobilize amount) turns reserves back
              into usable energy. These conversions are explicit actions. Linked
              sharing conserves existing reserves.
            

            

              Filling either pool becomes progressively less efficient as it
              grows. Its configurable fill scale sets how quickly intake slows;
              0 disables this effect. Energy and storage colors reach full
              brightness around three times their fill scale, independently of
              the generous safety limits. Hover over an inspected pool to see
              its current intake efficiency.
            

            

              (attack target amount) costs energy and strikes the
              target. Built barriers absorb damage before its energy is reduced.
              It transfers nothing. A corpse contains body material plus the
              victim's stored reserves; (eat) eats a random corpse
              within reach and returns the energy gained. Gold rings mark
              successful feeding.
            

            

              (shield amount) spends energy to build a persistent
              barrier. Violet rims show remaining barrier strength. Attacks wear
              it down; building more repairs it. Barrier capacity, toughness,
              construction efficiency and upkeep are configurable.
            

            

              (energy) and (storage) read your own
              pools. (sunlight-bearing) senses sunlight.
              (storage-bearing kind) points toward reserves: 0
              living, 1 corpses, −1 both.
              (alive target) distinguishes status.
