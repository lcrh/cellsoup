An open-ended experiment

            

              There is no scripted upgrade. Sterile and inefficient mutants can
              die out; successful variants propagate through division. The
              environment supplies food continuously.
            

            

              Random or archived genomes arrive steadily, with extra arrivals
              when populations are low. Resampling is the default source of
              mutation; ordinary division copies code exactly. Arrival counts
              and archive size are reported here. Disable arrivals in World to
              run a closed population.
            

          

        

        

          
Conditions in the dish

          
          

            The same seed, programs, settings, and sequence of interactions
            reproduce the same simulation.
          

          
          
          

            Max speed runs as fast as possible and skips intermediate drawings.
            Physics uses the same fixed timestep. Choose Max in the speed
            control above the dish.
          

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          

            Drops wander and fluctuate in amount with Ornstein–Uhlenbeck
            randomness. Longer memory means more persistent patches; the drop
            location drifts around a randomly placed home region. Zero range
            fixes its position; zero variation fixes its amount.
          

          
          

            Division copies the parent's code exactly at 0%. Resampled arrivals
            have a separate mutation rate below.
          

          
          
          

            Daughters inherit heading with a small random offset. This changes
            orientation only, independently of genome mutation.
          

          
Energy costs

          
          
          

            Charged even while waiting. At the default, an idle cell's initial
            70 energy lasts about 35 simulated seconds without food. Food
            absorption can offset upkeep (up to 9.6 energy per second).
            Voluntary actions cannot spend the last 0.001 energy; unaffordable
            actions are skipped. Shields switch off when their upkeep is
            unaffordable.
          

          

            Instruction and action costs
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            

              All actions also pay instruction cost. Division requires its
              configured cost plus 20 energy; the remainder is shared equally.
            

          

          
Spontaneous arrivals

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          

            The steady trickle continues above the replenishment threshold.
            Below it, up to 64 extra cells arrive per simulated second. Arrivals
            stop at the hard population limit. Each gets random or archived
            code, with random fallback when the archive is empty. Set both
            arrival controls to 0 to disable arrivals.
          

          

            A resampled mutation makes one code edit. The archive keeps up to
            128 variants that produced at least 3 offspring and persisted for 10
            seconds. Reset clears the archive. Random programs contain 8–64
            instructions.
          

          
          

            
A finite-energy ecology

            

              Food arrives in blobs and diffuses. Cells absorb it automatically.
              Bonds share energy; every instruction has a small metabolic cost.
            

            

              Division pays its configured cost and shares the remainder.
              Shields reduce theft by up to 90%. Death returns some embodied
              energy to the soup.
            

            

              The dish wraps at its edges. Physics runs at 60 ticks per
              simulated second.
