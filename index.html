An open-ended experiment

            

              There is no scripted upgrade. Sterile and inefficient mutants can
              die out; successful variants propagate through division. The
              environment supplies food continuously.
            

            

              Low populations are replenished with random or archived genomes.
              Resampling is the default source of mutation; ordinary division
              copies code exactly. Arrival counts and archive size are reported
              here. Disable arrivals in World to run a closed population.
            

          

        

        

          
Conditions in the dish

          
          

            The same seed, programs, settings, and sequence of interactions
            reproduce the same simulation.
          

          
          

            Division copies the parent's code exactly at 0%. Resampled arrivals
            have a separate mutation rate below.
          

          
Spontaneous arrivals

          
          
          
          
          
          
          

            Below the threshold, up to 64 cells arrive each simulated second.
            Each gets a fresh random program or a sampled archive genome. An
            empty archive falls back to random. Set the threshold to 0 to
            disable arrivals.
          

          

            A resampled mutation makes one code edit. The archive keeps up to
            128 variants that produced at least 3 offspring and persisted for 10
            seconds. Reset clears the archive. Random programs contain 8–64
            instructions.
          

          
          

            
A finite-energy ecology

            

              Food arrives in blobs and diffuses. Cells absorb it automatically.
              Bonds share energy; every instruction has a small metabolic cost.
            

            

              Division costs 12 energy and shares the remainder. Shields reduce
              theft by up to 90%. Death returns some embodied energy to the
              soup.
            

            

              The dish wraps at its edges. Physics runs at 60 ticks per
              simulated second.
